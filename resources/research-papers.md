# Research Papers

This curated index highlights influential research papers in distributed systems, reliability, observability, and AI infrastructure. Use this guide to find foundational and modern works that shape cloud-native engineering.

## How to Use This Index

- Papers are grouped by primary focus area.
- Each entry includes a rationale for inclusion, a focus tag, and a direct URL to the paper or its official publisher page.
- Selection emphasizes historical importance, modern relevance, and reproducibility.

## Selection Criteria

- Historical importance
- Relevance to modern cloud-native systems
- Reproducibility and citation quality
- Vendor-neutral insights

---

## Distributed Systems

- **["The Google File System" – Sanjay Ghemawat, Howard Gobioff, Shun-Tak Leung (2003)](https://research.google/pubs/pub51/)**  
  [Distributed Systems]  
  *Rationale:* Landmark paper on scalable, fault-tolerant storage, foundational for cloud-native storage architectures.

- **["MapReduce: Simplified Data Processing on Large Clusters" – Jeffrey Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat (2004)](https://research.google/pubs/pub62/)**  
  [Distributed Systems]  
  *Rationale:* Introduced the MapReduce paradigm, enabling scalable data processing in distributed environments.

## Reliability

- **["Dapper, a Large-Scale Distributed Systems Tracing Infrastructure" – Benjamin H. Sigelman et al. (2010)](https://research.google/pubs/pub36356/)**  
  [Observability]  
  *Rationale:* Influential work on distributed tracing, inspiring modern observability tools.

- **["Site Reliability Engineering: How Google Runs Production Systems" – Betsy Beyer et al. (2016)](https://sre.google/books/)**  
  [Reliability]  
  *Rationale:* SRE principles and practices, widely adopted for reliability engineering.

## AI Infrastructure

- **["Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems" – D. Sculley et al. (2015)](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/5656-hidden-technical-debt-in-machine-learning-systems.pdf)**  
  [AI Infrastructure]  
  *Rationale:* Seminal paper on the complexity and operational risks of production ML systems.
