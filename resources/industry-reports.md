# Industry Reports

This curated index tracks credible annual and periodic reports on software delivery, reliability, security, platform engineering, and AI/ML trends. Use this guide to identify durable, high-impact industry research for strategic and technical decision-making.

## How to Use This Index

- Reports are grouped by primary focus area.
- Each entry includes a rationale for inclusion, a focus tag, and a direct URL to the official report or publisher page.
- Selection emphasizes transparent methodology, enterprise relevance, and trend stability.

## Selection Criteria

- Transparent methodology
- Enterprise relevance
- Trend stability over time
- Vendor-neutral analysis
