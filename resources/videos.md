# Videos

This curated index includes technical talks and lecture series that complement this repository. Use this guide to find durable, high-impact video resources for cloud-native architecture, reliability, platform engineering, security, and AI/ML.

## How to Use This Index

- Videos are grouped by primary focus area.
- Each entry includes a rationale for inclusion, a focus tag, and a direct URL to the video or its official publisher page.
- Selection emphasizes conceptual framing, practical examples, and durable relevance.

## Selection Criteria

- Strong conceptual framing
- Useful architecture and operations examples
- Durable relevance beyond a specific tool release
- Vendor-neutral perspective
