# Books

This curated index includes foundational and advanced books relevant to cloud-native architecture, reliability, platform engineering, security, and related disciplines. Use this guide to identify durable, high-impact resources for both practitioners and leaders.

## How to Use This Index

- Books are grouped by primary focus area.
- Each entry includes a rationale for inclusion, a focus tag, and a direct URL to the book or its official publisher page.
- Selection emphasizes conceptual depth, practical engineering value, and long-term relevance.

## Selection Criteria

- Conceptual depth
- Practical engineering relevance
- Long-term durability
- Vendor-neutral perspective

---

## Architecture

- **["Building Microservices" – Sam Newman (2015, 2021)](https://samnewman.io/books/building_microservices/)**  
  [Architecture]  
  *Rationale:* Seminal guide to microservices design, patterns, and trade-offs for modern distributed systems.

- **["Cloud Native Patterns" – Cornelia Davis (2019)](https://www.manning.com/books/cloud-native-patterns)**  
  [Architecture]  
  *Rationale:* Practical patterns for designing resilient, scalable, and evolvable cloud-native systems.

## Reliability & Operations

- **["Site Reliability Engineering" – Betsy Beyer et al. (2016)](https://sre.google/books/)**  
  [Reliability]  
  *Rationale:* Foundational text on SRE principles, reliability trade-offs, and operational excellence.

- **["Release It!" – Michael T. Nygard (2018, 2nd Ed.)](https://pragprog.com/titles/mnee2/release-it-second-edition/)**  
  [Reliability]  
  *Rationale:* Classic work on designing systems for production resilience, including bulkheads, circuit breakers, and failure modes.

## Platform Engineering & DevOps

- **["Team Topologies" – Matthew Skelton & Manuel Pais (2019)](https://teamtopologies.com/book)**  
  [Platform Engineering]  
  *Rationale:* Influential framework for organizing teams and platforms for fast flow and reliability.

- **["The DevOps Handbook" – Gene Kim et al. (2016)](https://itrevolution.com/products/the-devops-handbook)**  
  [DevOps]  
  *Rationale:* Comprehensive guide to DevOps practices, automation, and cultural transformation.

## Security

- **["Securing DevOps" – Julien Vehent (2018)](https://www.manning.com/books/securing-devops)**  
  [Security]  
  *Rationale:* Practical strategies for integrating security into cloud-native and DevOps workflows.

## AI/ML in Cloud

- **["Designing Machine Learning Systems" – Chip Huyen (2022)](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-machine-learning/9781098107963/)**  
  [AI/ML]  
  *Rationale:* Modern guide to building reliable, scalable ML systems in cloud-native environments.
